Studio communication breaks down when the conversation is separated from the thing being discussed.

## Link discussions to records

A payment question should connect to the payment schedule. A shoot question should connect to the shoot. A delivery question should connect to the deliverable.

## Reduce repeated context

When the team can see the related booking, client, date, amount, or assignment, the conversation gets shorter and more useful.

## Keep decisions visible

Important decisions should not disappear into a chat scroll. They should remain close to the operational record they affect.
