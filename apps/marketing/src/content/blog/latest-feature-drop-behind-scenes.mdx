A studio dashboard should not be decorative. It should tell you where attention is needed.

## Upcoming work

Show shoots, events, and deliverables that are close enough to affect planning.

## Money at risk

Surface overdue payment schedules, unpaid invoices, received amounts, and expenses.

## Operational bottlenecks

Highlight tasks without owners, delivery work falling behind, and bookings missing crew.

## Fast paths

A useful dashboard lets you move from signal to action: open the booking, message the team, assign the crew, or update the payment.
