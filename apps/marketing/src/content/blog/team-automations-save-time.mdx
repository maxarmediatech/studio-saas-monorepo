Manual follow-up works until the studio gets busy. Then the missed reminders start costing money and trust.

## Payment due reminders

Upcoming and overdue payment schedules should be reviewed consistently.

## Crew confirmation

Assignments should be checked before every shoot day.

## Deliverable deadlines

Albums, edits, reels, and final exports need visible owners and dates.

## Client update points

Clients should receive updates before they need to ask.

## Expense review

Booking-related expenses should be captured before profitability becomes a guess.
