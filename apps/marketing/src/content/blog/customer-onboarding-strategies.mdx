Client onboarding sets the tone for the entire project. The goal is simple: capture the details once, make them easy to trust, and give every team member the same context.

## Capture the relationship map

For weddings and events, one booking may involve multiple people. Store names, phone numbers, email addresses, and relation types so the team knows who is the client, who approves decisions, and who should receive updates.

## Confirm the package and dates

Add package cost, booking type, event dates, venues, and shoot requirements before the team starts planning resources.

## Set payment expectations early

Create payment milestones at booking time. Clear due dates and amounts reduce uncomfortable follow-ups later.

## Turn promises into deliverables

Albums, edited films, reels, raw files, prints, and other commitments should become deliverables with owners and dates.
