Every studio has its own style, but the operational backbone is usually repeatable. The trick is to standardize the parts that should not require fresh thinking every time.

## Standardize booking stages

Use consistent statuses for inquiry, confirmed, shooting, editing, delivered, and archived work.

## Standardize payment milestones

Common templates like advance, pre-shoot balance, post-shoot payment, and final delivery payment help teams move faster.

## Standardize crew roles

Lead photographer, second shooter, cinematographer, editor, assistant, and album designer can be configured once and reused.

## Keep room for creativity

Operational consistency protects creative energy. It gives the team fewer details to chase and more space to focus on the client experience.
