Studio teams are naturally distributed. Crew may be on location, editors may be remote, and managers may be juggling client calls.

## Give every role the right context

Field crew need shoot dates, venues, roles, and call details. Editors need deliverables, deadlines, and client expectations. Managers need payment and status visibility.

## Assign ownership clearly

Every task and deliverable should have an owner. Shared responsibility often turns into no responsibility.

## Use one operational record

When everyone works from the same booking and shoot records, fewer details get lost between teams.
