Creative work can look busy while cash flow quietly gets messy. Real-time financial visibility helps studio owners see the difference between booked revenue and collected money.

## Track expected money

Payment schedules show what should be collected and when.

## Track received money

Received amounts show what actually arrived, which invoice or schedule it belongs to, and whether proof is attached.

## Track money leaving

Expenses tied to bookings make profitability easier to understand.

## Review overdue items weekly

Overdue schedules should be visible before they become uncomfortable client conversations.
