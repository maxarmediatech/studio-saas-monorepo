A wedding booking can span months of coordination. A clear workflow keeps it from becoming a collection of reminders.

## Booking details

Start with the client, package, event dates, venues, and notes.

## Shoot plan

Create separate shoots for pre-wedding, ceremony, reception, and any add-on sessions.

## Crew plan

Assign leads, assistants, cinematographers, and editors with rates where needed.

## Finance plan

Add payment milestones and invoices before the team gets deep into production.

## Delivery plan

Turn every promise into a deliverable with an owner and expected date.
