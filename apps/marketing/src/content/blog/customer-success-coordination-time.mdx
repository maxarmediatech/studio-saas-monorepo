Most studio coordination problems are not caused by laziness. They happen because the truth is split between people, messages, spreadsheets, and memory.

## The common failure pattern

A coordinator tracks dates in a calendar. The owner tracks payments in a spreadsheet. Crew assignments live in chat. Deliverables live in someone else's notes. Everyone is working, but nobody has the full picture.

## The better pattern

Connect each piece to the booking: shoots, crew assignments, payment schedules, invoices, receipts, expenses, tasks, deliverables, and discussions.

## What improves first

Daily planning gets faster. Payment follow-up becomes clearer. Crew questions reduce. Delivery promises are easier to track. The studio stops reassembling context before every decision.
