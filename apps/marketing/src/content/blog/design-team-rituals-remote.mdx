A studio does not need more meetings. It needs a small set of rituals that reveal the work that needs attention.

## Morning operations review

Check upcoming shoots, open tasks, overdue payments, and deliverables due this week.

## Crew confirmation

Before busy shoot days, confirm assignments, venues, times, and responsibilities.

## Finance follow-up

Review payment schedules and received amounts so client communication stays timely.

## Delivery review

Look at edits, albums, exports, approvals, and handoffs before they become urgent.
